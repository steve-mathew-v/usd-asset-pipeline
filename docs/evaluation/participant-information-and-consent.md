# Participant Information & Consent

## About this study

You are invited to take part in a short study evaluating a **USD asset
pipeline** — a tool that lets artists share 3D assets between DCC applications
(Maya, Houdini) through a central server, with version history.

This study is part of an MSc Computer Animation & Visual Effects project at the
NCCA, Bournemouth University.

## What you'll be asked to do

- Answer a few quick background questions.
- Carry out a set of short tasks using the tool (publishing, approving and
  importing an asset, and choosing a version), taking about 20 minutes.
- Fill in a short usability questionnaire and give some open feedback.

The whole session takes about **25–30 minutes**.

## Is it voluntary?

Yes. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You can skip any question and stop at
any time, without giving a reason and without any consequence.

## What is being recorded?

- Whether each task is completed, roughly how long it takes, and any points
  where you needed help.
- Your questionnaire answers and comments.

No video or audio is recorded unless you are told otherwise and agree
separately. **Your name is not attached to the data** — responses are stored
against an anonymous ID (P1, P2, …).

## How your data is used

Results are used only for this academic project. Only **aggregated results and
anonymised quotes** appear in the report. Data is stored securely and not shared
outside the project.
