## Scoring (for the researcher — do not show participants)

The 10 items alternate in direction; convert each to a 0–4 "score
contribution":

- **Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9):** contribution = (answer − 1)
- **Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10):** contribution = (5 − answer)

Sum the 10 contributions (range 0–40), then **multiply by 2.5** to get the
final SUS score out of **100**.

**Interpreting the score (rough benchmarks):**

| SUS score | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------------|
| 85–100 | Excellent |
| 68–84 | Good / above average |
| ~68 | Average |
| 51–67 | Below average |
| < 51 | Poor |

Report the **mean** SUS across participants and the **range**. (SUS gives a
single usability number; it is not a percentage.)
